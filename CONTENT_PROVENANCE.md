# Content provenance and copyright review checklist

This file is an editorial control, not a legal guarantee. A public website can still receive a copyright complaint even when it has a copyright policy and contributor checkbox.

## Before publishing any bundled content

For every note, question set, syllabus transcription, image, and downloadable file, record:

1. **Creator / rights holder** — who wrote or created it.
2. **Source** — exact source URL, book title/edition/page, or original authoring record.
3. **Rights basis** — original work, written permission, public domain, or the exact open licence and version.
4. **Attribution** — required author, title, source, licence link, and change notice.
5. **Review date and reviewer** — when it was checked and by whom.

Do not mark material `Original StudySphere` solely because it is stored in this repository or was generated with an AI tool. Verify that it does not reproduce protected expression from a textbook, coaching booklet, website, or exam paper.

## Public note contributions

The upload form offers CC BY-SA 4.0 and CC BY 4.0. Accept a contribution only when the contributor owns the rights or has authority to apply that licence. Preserve contributor identity/credit and the source/permission record needed to respond to a dispute.

## Practice papers and PYQs

Do not upload scans, photographs, copied question sets, or official examination papers unless redistribution is expressly permitted. Prefer original practice questions and links to the official source. A publicly downloadable paper is not automatically reusable.

## Third-party material

Prefer links to official portals. Before mirroring any document or image, confirm the specific licence or written permission covers redistribution and any required attribution. Keep a copy of the licence/permission evidence.

## Takedown workflow

1. Record the complaint and the exact affected URL/material.
2. Temporarily unpublish the disputed item while reviewing a credible claim.
3. Preserve the complaint and relevant provenance records.
4. Verify the claimant, rights, and contributor response.
5. Remove or restore the item based on the review and document the decision.

The current app stores public submissions immediately after upload and does not provide a built-in moderation dashboard. Treat that as a launch blocker for accepting unrestricted public contributions: manually review submissions and/or implement an approval queue before promoting uploads widely.
